# Phase 5: red team, fixes and final report

The red team played two people from the brief: a skeptical first-time investor and an Apple App Review reviewer. They looked for confusion, battery drain, dark patterns and anything that reads like investment advice. Every finding came from an outside instrument, not my own judgment:
- **axe-core**, the standard accessibility checker (WCAG 2.2 A and AA plus best practices)
- **the live contrast audit**, now able to read gradients
- **the color-blindness check** (`scripts/palette-check.mjs`)
- **a sweep of every main screen** (`e2e/redteam.spec.ts`), checking touch targets, endless animations, sideways scrolling and clipped text at normal and large text sizes
- **a scan of all 734 card texts in the database** for advice words, hype and unexplained acronyms
- **Ask AI** asked five leading questions through the deployed function

Raw results: `docs/phase5/sweep-before.json` and `sweep-after.json`. Screens: `docs/phase5/screens/` (each screen at normal and large text).

## What the red team found, and what was done

**Accessibility (VoiceOver and touch):**
- **Fixed.** The Profile back button had no name, and the Name and Email fields had no labels (axe: critical).
- **Fixed.** The company details sheet had no name for screen readers. The company name is now its title, and it wraps instead of being cut off.
- **Fixed.** The cause buttons' spoken names didn't match their visible text (14 cases). They now read the visible label, then ", pick 1".
- **Fixed.** No main landmark, and screens without a top-level heading. Each screen now has both, and the Ask AI panel is a named region.
- **Fixed.** Undersized touch targets:
  - The "What is P/E Ratio?" help buttons (16 px) on every card.
  - The 16 px close button on every sheet and dialog, the paywall included. The brief forbids a tiny close button, and this was one.
  - "Show password" (16 px), the card's details button, Ask AI's close, send, suggestions and "Step by step", Likes' Details, the news pill, the details tabs and the coach mark's "Got it".
  - A new `tap-target` utility grows the tappable area to 46 px without changing the look.
- **Fixed.** In the details sheet, the price sat under the close button.

**First-time investor:**
- **Fixed.** On the causes screen, only 4½ of the 7 causes fit an iPhone 15, and the descriptions were cut off ("Cancer care, early detection and vac…"). All 7 now fit, with full descriptions, on an iPhone 15 and up. A test checks this at the in-app size, 393 × 759.
- **Fixed.** The swipe card cut text mid-line with no sign there was more below. A soft fade now shows there's more to scroll. It's pure CSS, so swiping stays smooth.
- **Fixed.** The small "Proof", "Things to know" and "Big swings" labels were at 4.39:1 while the card faded in, just under the 4.5:1 minimum. They now use the darkest tone, at 7.2:1 and up.
- **Needs your yes: 30 acronyms on company cards aren't spelled out.** These are database edits:
  - FDA on 17 cards, plus TSA, FTC, SEC, DEA, CPAP, RSV, RNAi, EV, PFAS, ASABE, UV and USAID.
  - The Clean energy description says "EVs".
  - Each edit is written and checked in `docs/phase5/acronyms.sql`, generated by `acronyms.mjs`. A dry run confirmed each of the 32 edits changes exactly one row.
  - Product names (Case IH, VAX-31, BIG-IP) and everyday terms (AI, DNA, COVID, GPS, CT) are left as they are.
- **Clean: no advice language in the app or the data.** All six "buy/sell" hits in the data are facts about acquisitions ("agreed to buy Penumbra"). The 29 superlatives are sourced size facts ("the largest U.S. water utility").

## iOS text size (Dynamic Type)

`src/lib/textSize.ts`:
- **How it reads the setting:** the iPhone app reads the text size you set in iOS (through `-apple-system-body`) and scales the whole app, since all type and spacing are in rem.
- **Default:** unchanged (16 px root at iOS "Large").
- **Larger sizes:** grow the app up to 1.25× (xLarge 18 px, xxxLarge and above 20 px). It updates when you come back from Settings.
- **At 18 px and up:** the deck switches to a roomier layout. Your cause chips share one sideways-scrolling row, and the swipe hint steps aside (the buttons say the same), so the card keeps its story on screen. Every screen was checked at 20 px for overflow and clipping.
- **Not on the website,** because desktop Safari reports a smaller system size.

**The cap:** iOS's largest accessibility sizes (up to 53 px) stop at 1.25× here. A full-screen swipe card can't hold 3× text, so they don't scale further.
